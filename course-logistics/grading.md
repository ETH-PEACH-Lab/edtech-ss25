# Grading

The grading consists of individual components and group project components:

* Individual components
  * In-class quizzes (10%)
  * Short essay (10%)
  * Evaluation plan (10%)
* Project components
  * Project proposal (10%)
  * Paper prototype (20%)
  * Demo day (10%)
  * Final deliverables (30%)
