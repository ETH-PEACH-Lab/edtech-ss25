# Project Theme

## ETH Zürich Campus Heilbronn

ETH Zurich is expanding its presence with [a new campus in Heilbronn](https://ethz.ch/en/the-eth-zurich/global/global-initiatives/campus-heilbronn.html), scheduled to launch its first degree programs in 2027. This creates a unique opportunity to design tools and experiences that will shape the academic and social life of students, faculty, and researchers across both campuses.



Imagine what campus life will look like between ETH Heilbronn and ETH Zurich. How can we create an interconnected academic ecosystem where students, instructors, and researchers feel engaged, supported, and connected regardless of physical location?&#x20;



Your project should focus on one of the following themes:\


**Theme 1: Facilitating Hybrid Learning**

* Design solutions to ensure students at both campuses can equally access lectures, tutorials, and other learning experiences.
* Imagine how a hybrid classroom may look—we encourage you to be more creative than the typical Zoom-based solutions. We all know how challenging and impersonal remote learning felt during the pandemic.\


**Theme 2: Enabling Collaborative Research**

* Design tools that allow researchers at Heilbronn and Zurich to collaborate seamlessly on projects.
* Consider ideas such as shared digital workspaces, virtual research labs, or methods for embodied collaboration across locations.



**Theme 3: Building a Connected Campus Community**

* Propose platforms that foster student engagement, networking, and social activities across campuses.
* Focus on creating digital spaces for informal learning, mentoring, and student organizations that span both locations.
